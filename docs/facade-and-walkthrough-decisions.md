facade to be like the file I attached with villa architecture. Modern design."
The walkthrough is deliverable 2 and Phase 4 of CLAUDE.md. Questions are asked
one at a time; every answer is recorded here, newest last, and logged in
docs/decisions.md once the set is complete.
