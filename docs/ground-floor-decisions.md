# Ground floor: the owner's answers, 2026-10-02

CONCEPT DESIGN, NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION.

On 2026-10-02 Aiman rejected the generated plans (band layout D-026 to D-029,
family hall D-032, central hall D-036) and restarted the design from the
beginning, one floor at a time, answering one question at a time. The ground
floor comes first. Each answer below is an owner decision and is logged in
docs/decisions.md as D-038 onward once the floor is complete. Earlier
decisions stand only where these answers do not replace them.

Frame used in the questions: road (side 1, WNW) on the west, landscape plot
(side 3, ESE) on the east, neighbour 5130271 (side 4) north, neighbour 5130123
(side 2) south. Buildable envelope inside the 3 m setbacks about 24.5 m road to
landscape by 27.7 m neighbour to neighbour. GFA cap 662.99 m2 (7,136 sq ft).
