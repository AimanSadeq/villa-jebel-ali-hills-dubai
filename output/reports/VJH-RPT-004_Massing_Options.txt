WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY

Option A, Garden East:
  The villa stands west of the pool, so its shadow falls across the water
  through the afternoon: 66% shaded at 15:00, 99% by 16:00 and complete from
  17:00 to sunset. Mean 93% across the cooling season. In winter the sun
  swings far enough south to pass the mass entirely, leaving 100% sun on the
  water at midday. Garden and pool face the landscape plot, so no neighbour
  overlooks them.

Option B, Garden Southwest:
  The villa sits north-east of the pool. Afternoon shadows run east-south-
  east, along the pool rather than across it, so the deck gets no shade at
  all from 15:00 to 18:00. The only shaded hour is 19:00, at 60%, by which
  point the sun is nearly down and the shade is worth little. Mean 12%. The
  pool also sits against neighbour plot 5130123 rather than the open
  landscape plot, so it is the least private of the three. Tested because
  CLAUDE.md asks for it. It is clearly the weakest.

Option C, Courtyard:
  The L wraps the two hot faces and shades well, 87% mean, within six points
  of option A. The cost shows up in two places. In winter the same wing that
  blocks the summer afternoon sun also blocks the low winter sun, leaving
  78% sun on the water at midday against 100% for option A. And at 15:00,
  the hour the deck is most likely to be in use before evening, it manages
  44% against 66% for option A. It also turns the garden inward and gives up
  the view east. Worth keeping if privacy from the road ever matters more
  than the view.

RECOMMENDATION

Option A, Garden East. It holds 93% of the pool deck in shade through
cooling season afternoons, never dropping below 61%, while still giving
100% sun on the water at winter midday. It is the only option that wins on
both, because the villa stands between the pool and the afternoon sun
without standing between the pool and the winter sun.

It also puts the garden and the main glazing on the ESE face toward
landscape plot 5130274, which the sun study shows is the cheapest face to
glaze and which carries the plot's only permanent open view. The master
suite can take that orientation, as CLAUDE.md section 4 hoped it would.
The hot WNW road face and the SSW neighbour face are given the service
rooms, the garage and deep overhangs rather than glass.

Option B, Garden Southwest, is the option CLAUDE.md asked to be tested
against it, and it fails on the single measure that matters most here: 12%
afternoon shade. The villa sits north-east of the pool, so its shadow
travels east-south-east away from the water rather than across it. The
deck would be in full sun every summer afternoon, and the pool is also the
side overlooked by neighbour plot 5130123.
