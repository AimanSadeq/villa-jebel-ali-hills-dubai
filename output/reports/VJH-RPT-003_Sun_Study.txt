At 24.9 N the summer sun passes nearly overhead, so at noon it is roofs and
not walls that take the load. The problem is the low afternoon sun in the
west, which strikes glass almost head on while the air is at its hottest.

Totals alone are misleading. Peak cooling demand in Dubai is late
afternoon, when ambient air is at its hottest, so a kWh arriving at 17:00
costs the plant far more than the same kWh at 08:00. Read the am and pm
columns, not the summer total.

Side 3, the landscape plot face, takes 368 kWh/m2 in summer mornings against only 51 in the afternoons.
Side 1, the road face, takes 233 kWh/m2 in summer afternoons, 4.6 times side 3.

So the east face, which is also the face with the permanent open view and
no overlooking neighbour, is the cheap one to glaze. The west and
south-west faces are the expensive ones and need deep shading.
